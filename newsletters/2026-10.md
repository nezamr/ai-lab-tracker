# AI Lab Tracker: October 2026

Issue #2. October was about new model tiers and open weights. Google split Gemini 4 into Argon and Carbon, OpenAI filled in GPT-6 with a cheaper Terra, and Anthropic shipped Haiku 5.5 along with a long list of Claude app and Enterprise features. Open weights kept coming from every direction: Mistral Large 4, Meta's Muse Spark Open, MiniMax M3.1 Flash, Step 5, a run of Nemotron 3.5 models, and Reflection AI's first model, Beam.

## Headlines

- 🆕 **Gemini 4 Argon → Carbon**: Argon went GA on Oct 13, two weeks after its trusted-tester debut. Carbon, now Google's strongest model, followed on Oct 28 for cyber defenders. [source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/)
- 🔄 **GPT-6 for everyone + GPT-6.1 Terra**: GPT-6 Sol/Luna became the ChatGPT default on all plans, and Terra costs half as much as 6.1 Sol in the API. [source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-terra/)
- 🆕 **Claude Haiku 5.5**: 1M context at $0.10/$0.50 per M tokens, about 75% cheaper to run than Haiku 4.5. [source](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5)
- 🆕 **Meta Muse Wonder**: Meta's first frontier models, followed three days later by the 816B open-weight Muse Spark Open. [source](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/10/introducing-muse-wonder-models/)
- 🆕 **Kling 4.0**: single-pass video up to 30 seconds with 15-asset Omni Reference. [source](https://ir.kuaishou.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kling-ai-officially-launches-next-generation-kling-40-raising)

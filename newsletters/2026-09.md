# AI Lab Tracker: September 2026

Issue #1. September was all about agents. OpenAI launched always-on personal agents (Dots) at DevDay, Meta shipped its Muse agent and new hardware to run it on, and Anthropic folded Cowork into the main Claude app. Meanwhile, model release cycles shrank to weeks: GPT-6 Astra, Sol and Luna, Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5, Gemini 3.8 Flash, and Grok 4.7 all shipped in one month.

## Headlines

- 🆕 **OpenAI Dots**: always-on personal agents, each with its own cloud computer and browser, rolling out to Pro and Business Premium. [source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/)
- 🔄 **GPT-6 Astra → Sol/Luna → 6.1 Sol**: three GPT-6 releases in four weeks; 6.1 Sol reaches near-Astra quality at about ⅕ the price. [source](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents-cloud-computers-devday)
- 🔄 **Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5**: Opus 5.5 matches Fable 5.1 on most work at about 40% lower cost. [source](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5)
- 🆕 **Meta Muse agent + Connect 2026 hardware**: a personal agent, the Muse Charm handheld, and new Ray-Ban glasses. [source](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/the-biggest-news-from-connect-2026/)
- 🪦 **Sora shut down**: OpenAI discontinued the Sora 2 video models and the Videos API. [source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001152-what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation)
